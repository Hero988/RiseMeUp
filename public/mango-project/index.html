Where this fits

One crop.
A wider purpose.

Virgin Mary Farm is the main project: an integrated plan for agricultural recovery, food security, clean energy and skills.

The Mango Project is a supporting part of that ambition. It explores how improved crop handling, sorting, packing and treatment could help farmers retain more value from their harvests.

Sudan’s war has damaged livelihoods and infrastructure. Virgin Mary Farm is not currently operational. The Mango Project would need to be assessed and developed within the wider rebuilding programme, alongside the needs of the Coptic Orthodox and Nuba Mountains communities we support.

The opportunity

From harvest
to lasting value.

The original proposal identified a need for better post-harvest infrastructure. The next step is to validate that opportunity with growers, technical specialists and potential buyers.

Reduce avoidable losses

Assess crop protection, careful handling, sorting and temperature management to help preserve fruit quality.

Develop skills & work

Explore training and local employment across growing, grading, packing, maintenance and logistics.

Build market connections

Understand local and regional demand first, then assess export opportunities and each market’s requirements.

The proposed facility

Good infrastructure.
At every step.

The concept combines a packing centre with a specialist treatment system, appropriate storage and a practical route to market. Treatment methods would be selected with qualified specialists and the relevant authorities.

Before procurement, the plan needs a current assessment of water, energy, site access, crop supply, maintenance, operating costs and buyer requirements. No export approval, organic certification or guaranteed market access is claimed.

  1. 01 / Receive

    Receive fruit, record its origin and inspect quality.

  2. 02 / Prepare

    Sort and grade fruit, with washing and treatment as appropriate.

  3. 03 / Pack

    Pack, label and store fruit under suitable conditions.

  4. 04 / Connect

    Coordinate dispatch, transport and buyer documentation.

Illustrative workflow only. The final design must follow the selected crops, treatment protocol and destination-market requirements.
