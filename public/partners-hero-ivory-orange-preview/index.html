Virgin Mary Farm · For development partners

Rebuilding starts
with water.

Help women rebuild their lives through sustainable farming, education and opportunities to earn a living in Sudan.

Virgin Mary Farm is Risemeup’s main development project in Omdurman, Khartoum. Our founder reports that the farm’s seven wells were destroyed and all pumping equipment stolen. Our first proposed step is to assess the damage, establish a viable water source and build new water storage for an initial growing and training area.

Rebuilding brief · 21 October 2026 · Version 8
Planning and assessment stage; not yet ready for equipment delivery.

A practical path to recovery

  1. Assess.

    Confirm the wells, land-use arrangements, safe access and local delivery team.

  2. Restore.

    Design and cost well rehabilitation or replacement, pumping and new water storage, subject to assessment.

  3. Grow.

    Start cultivation and practical training, document progress, then plan expansion.

Together with the Coptic Orthodox and Nuba Mountains communities.
