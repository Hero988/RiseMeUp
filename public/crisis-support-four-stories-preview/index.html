Crisis support · Four stories of practical care

Practical help in times of crisis

Two places.
One commitment to help.

From food assistance in London and Sudan to care for children before the war and ongoing help reaching safer areas, Risemeup stands alongside people facing hardship.

01 / London

Food bank & homeless shelter support.

During the coronavirus pandemic, Iman helped deliver food to a food bank and a homeless shelter in London.

Her contribution centred on practical help: getting food to places supporting people who needed it.

02 / Sudan

Food aid for families.

Alongside her work in London, Iman coordinated food aid for families in Sudan who were facing hardship during the coronavirus crisis.

The response reflected the same commitment across both places: helping people access food in a time of need.

03 / Sudan · Wartime support

Helping people reach safety.

As the war continues in Sudan, Risemeup helps vulnerable people leave war zones and reach safer areas.

When conflict puts lives at risk, helping people reach a safer place is a vital expression of care and solidarity.

04 / Sudan · Before the war

Care for children without a home.

Before the war in Sudan, Risemeup supported children experiencing homelessness with blankets in winter, slippers in summer and a meal once a month.

Seasonal essentials and a regular meal offered practical care for children facing hardship.
