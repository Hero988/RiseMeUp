Who owns the land?
Founder-reported · 130 hectares

Iman Tadros confirms that the farm covers 130 hectares (approximately 321 acres) and identifies the landowner as the Coptic Orthodox Church in Sudan, Khartoum–Omdurman. Ownership documents, surveyed boundaries and the authorised Church signatory remain subject to partner due diligence. Cultivable areas and infrastructure allocations will follow site assessment.

Technical assessment

Current access

Permission to use the land does not establish that access or delivery routes are currently safe. Water availability
Wells destroyed · New water storage needed

Our founder reports that all seven wells have been destroyed and all pumping equipment stolen. New water storage needs to be built. No operational water supply is confirmed. A qualified assessment must determine whether wells can be rehabilitated or require replacement, and establish water availability and quality before the system is designed.

Secure storage and custody arrangements must be assessed and established before equipment is delivered. Detailed security information and exact storage locations will not be published.
