The communities we stand with

Standing with Sudan’s Coptic Orthodox, Nuba Mountains and Blue Nile communities through food assistance and a shared commitment to recovery.

Belonging · Sudan

Coptic Orthodoxcommunity

Sudan’s Coptic Orthodox families are part of the country’s social and spiritual life. War has separated families, disrupted livelihoods and threatened churches and community spaces.

This is our founder’s own community. Risemeup brings that personal connection to practical support for families facing displacement and uncertainty.

Resilience · South Kordofan

Nuba Mountainscommunities

The Nuba Mountains are home to diverse communities with deep ties to land and farming. Decades of conflict and displacement have interrupted education, food production and everyday life.

Our commitment is to help people rebuild through food security, practical training and opportunities to earn a living.

Dignity · Blue Nile

Blue Nilecommunities

Blue Nile’s communities have endured attacks on civilians, destroyed villages and forced displacement. Conflict has uprooted families from their homes and farmland, disrupting food supplies, education and community life.

Risemeup stands alongside them, recognising their suffering and their right to safety, dignity and a voice in shaping their future.

Our shared purpose

Food, skills and the opportunity to rebuild.

Our commitment embraces all three communities. Alongside food assistance, our vision for Virgin Mary Farm brings together sustainable farming, clean energy, education and practical training, with women affected by war at the heart of the planned rebuilding.

Historical background for the Blue Nile introduction

Amnesty International’s 2013 Blue Nile report documents war crimes, attacks on civilians and denial of humanitarian access. Its accompanying account describes destroyed villages and forced displacement. This background concerns documented events from the conflict beginning in 2011.
