Our goals

Strategic Objectives

Six connected ambitions guiding the rebuilding of Virgin Mary Farm and our work with communities.

Green energy

Develop solar power and biogas to support farming and make productive use of organic by-products.

Food security

Combine immediate food assistance with planned crop and poultry production for a more secure future.

New jobs

Create opportunities across growing, processing, packing, maintenance and distribution as the farm develops.

Ending poverty

Help families build the skills and opportunities to earn a living and become more self-reliant.

Education & training

Bring sustainable farming education and hands-on training into every part of the project.

Rural farming

Support practical, resource-conscious agriculture rooted in community knowledge and local needs.

Committed to grow rural communities.
