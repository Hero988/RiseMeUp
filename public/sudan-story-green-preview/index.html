The war in Sudan

Homes lost.
Churches burned.
Communities uprooted.

Since April 2023, war has torn apart families, livelihoods and places of refuge across Sudan.

For the communities we support, the loss is deeply personal. Families have been displaced, food and income have become uncertain, and churches have been damaged, burned, destroyed or occupied. These are places of worship, belonging and mutual support; their loss reaches far beyond the buildings.

The Coptic Orthodox community and the people of the Nuba Mountains have endured immense suffering, often with little international attention. Their stories, dignity and future must not be forgotten.

Background: UN OCHA’s Sudan response · CSW reporting on attacks on churches
