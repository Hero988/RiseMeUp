Our Vision

A future where communities affected by war can live with dignity, grow nutritious food and build lasting livelihoods.

Where land flourishes, opportunity is shared and families have the freedom to rebuild their lives.

Thriving land. Stronger communities. Renewed hope.

The work that guides us

Our Mission

To stand with Sudan’s Coptic Orthodox and Nuba Mountains communities through food assistance today and opportunities for tomorrow.

With our partners, we aim to rebuild Virgin Mary Farm as a place for sustainable farming, clean energy, learning and practical training.
