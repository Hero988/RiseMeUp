Budget statusAssessment comes first.

No quotation-backed funding total has been set.

We are seeking technical partners to help assess the site and develop a realistic first-stage budget. No quotation-backed funding total has been set.

Our first priority is a viable water supply, new storage and an initial growing and training area. The agreed budget will also cover delivery, installation, operator training, maintenance and contingency, including in-kind support.

Once the scope and costs have been validated, we plan to publish a dated budget summary and report how agreed support is used. We will not present preliminary machinery prices as confirmed costs.

Responsible transparency

What we publish and what we keep private

Public: our project purpose, readiness, equipment priorities, intended ownership, milestones and community-accountability approach. A validated budget summary and progress reports will be added as the project develops.

Private partner review: original supplier quotations, detailed cost schedules and supporting land-use or donation agreements, when available and with appropriate consent and redaction.

Bank details, signatures, beneficiaries’ personal information and sensitive storage or delivery arrangements will not be published. Contact Iman to discuss which documents are available and a suitable review process.

This protects people and commercially sensitive details without replacing financial accountability or any agreed donor-reporting requirements.
