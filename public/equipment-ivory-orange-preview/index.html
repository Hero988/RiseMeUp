Water comes first · provisional assessment-led needs below

01

Well restoration and pumping equipment

1 initial water-source system, subject to technical assessment

Awaiting quotation

Rehabilitate or replace a destroyed well as advised, and establish water supply for an initial cultivation and training area.

Required specification, operation and cost
Specification to confirm
Assess the destroyed wells to determine rehabilitation or replacement works, water availability and water quality. Specify the well works and then select pump type, flow, total head and power. Include rising main, cables, protection, fittings and a water meter; sizes to be confirmed.
Operator & maintenance
Proposed: a qualified well contractor for restoration and commissioning, then a trained pump operator with supplier servicing. Farm manager Jhone Wadeea is the local management contact; technical operators and maintenance providers remain to be confirmed.
Estimated cost
Awaiting assessment and supplier quotation. No amount established.
02

Solar power and pump controls

1 matched power package for the initial pump

Awaiting quotation

Provide power for the restored water-pumping system, subject to technical suitability.

Required specification, operation and cost
Specification to confirm
Panel capacity and controller matched to the selected pump and site conditions; secure mounting, cabling, earthing and electrical protection. No power rating specified before design.
Operator & maintenance
Proposed: the trained pump operator for routine checks and a qualified solar technician for servicing. Appointments and service arrangements pending.
Estimated cost
Awaiting assessment and supplier quotation. No amount established.
03

New water storage and filtration

Awaiting quotation

Build new water storage for irrigation and protect the irrigation system from blockage.

Required specification, operation and cost
Specification to confirm
New storage is required. Tank numbers, volume, foundations or supports, filters, valves and pipe sizes must follow the site assessment, water testing and demand calculations. Irrigation use; drinking-water suitability is not established.
Operator & maintenance
Proposed: a trained irrigation caretaker with supplier support and a cleaning schedule. Named responsibility and maintenance budget pending.
Estimated cost
Awaiting assessment and supplier quotation. No amount established.
