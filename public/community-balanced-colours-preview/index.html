Sudan’s Coptic Orthodox families are part of the country’s social and spiritual life. War has separated families, disrupted livelihoods and threatened churches and community spaces.

Nuba Mountainscommunities

The Nuba Mountains in South Kordofan are home to diverse communities with deep ties to land and farming. Decades of conflict and displacement have interrupted education, food production and everyday life.

Our original vision grew from a commitment to these communities: helping people rebuild through food security, training and opportunities to earn a living.
