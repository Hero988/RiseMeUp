Helping during coronavirus

Two places.
One commitment to help.

Food assistance connected Iman’s community work in London with support for families in Sudan during the pandemic.

01 / London

Food bank &
homeless shelter support.

During the coronavirus pandemic, Iman helped deliver food to a food bank and a homeless shelter in London.

Her contribution centred on practical help: getting food to places supporting people who needed it.

02 / Sudan

Food aid
for families.

Alongside her work in London, Iman coordinated food aid for families in Sudan who were facing hardship during the coronavirus crisis.

The response reflected the same commitment across both places: helping people access food in a time of need.

Think globally, act locally.

The commitment continues

Food today.
A future to rebuild.

The coronavirus response is part of Risemeup’s history. Today, our work includes food assistance for families affected by the war in Sudan, alongside the planned rebuilding of Virgin Mary Farm.

We support Sudan’s Coptic Orthodox and Nuba Mountains communities through immediate help and a long-term commitment to sustainable farming, education and livelihoods.
