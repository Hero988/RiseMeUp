The story behind the photographs.

What this archive records

These seven photographs were supplied by founder Iman Tadros as images of the London coronavirus response and food support in Sudan. Captions describe what is visible; the general locations and activity context come from her account.

Iman describes collecting and delivering food to a London food bank and homeless shelter during the pandemic, and coordinating food assistance for families in Sudan. The Sudan photographs are presented as a food-support archive; their exact dates have not been established.

What still needs confirmation

Exact photograph dates, detailed locations, distribution totals and independently verified results are not yet available for publication. The photographs alone do not establish how many people received assistance or when.

Records, dated activity accounts and references from organisations involved will be added where they can be verified and permission to publish is available. Organisation names and endorsements are not claimed without that confirmation.

The commitment continues

Food today.
A future to rebuild.

The coronavirus response is part of Risemeup’s history. Today, our work includes food assistance for families affected by the war in Sudan, alongside the planned rebuilding of Virgin Mary Farm.

We support Sudan’s Coptic Orthodox and Nuba Mountains communities through immediate help and a long-term commitment to sustainable farming, education and livelihoods.
