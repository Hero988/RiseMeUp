Our planned contribution to the SDGs

Shared goals.
Measurable local progress.

Baseline measurements, realistic targets and reporting responsibilities will be agreed during assessment. The measures below describe what we propose to track; they are not achieved results.

Goal 7Affordable and clean energy

Power for water

Proposed measure: renewable electricity generated in kWh and the share of pumping energy supplied by solar.

First-stage target: to be set after system design.
Goal 9Industry, innovation and infrastructure

A working first system

Proposed measure: wells commissioned, cubic metres of water delivered and hectares irrigated.

Proposed initial output: one operational water source following well rehabilitation or replacement, subject to viability; area and flow targets pending.
Goal 10Reduced inequalities

Skills and participation

Proposed measure: training enrolment, completion and access to livelihood opportunities, with voluntary, appropriate records of participation across the communities.

Targets for women, young people and displaced families to be agreed through consultation.
Goal 12Responsible consumption and production

Use resources well

Proposed measure: kilograms of organic material reused and produce losses. Wider biogas and fertiliser measures apply only when those facilities operate.

Baseline and targets to be agreed for the first growing cycle.

SDG alignment is Risemeup’s own planning framework. It is not United Nations approval, certification or endorsement. These are proposed project measures, not official UN indicators.
