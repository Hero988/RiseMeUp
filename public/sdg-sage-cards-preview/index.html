Four global goals.

Risemeup is committed to using the UN Sustainable Development Goals as a framework for rebuilding Virgin Mary Farm. These four goals connect our original proposal with practical action for communities in Sudan.

United Nations Sustainable Development Goals
07

Affordable and Clean Energy

Power the farm sustainably.

Planned solar systems and a biogas plant would provide renewable energy for farming, using organic by-products as a resource.

UN target 7.2More renewable energy↗
How progress could be measured

Renewable electricity generated and the share of farm energy supplied by solar and biogas.

09

Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure

Rebuild the foundations.

Ten greenhouses, irrigation, and sorting and packing facilities are planned to support productive farming and local enterprise.

UN target 9.1Sustainable infrastructure↗
How progress could be measured

Facilities completed and operational, irrigation installed, and produce-handling capacity available to the project.

10

Reduced Inequalities

Create opportunity together.

Sustainable farming education and livelihood opportunities would prioritise women affected by war, including widows and mothers raising children alone, across both communities.

UN target 10.2Social and economic inclusion↗
How progress could be measured

Participation in training, completion of courses, and access to livelihood opportunities for the communities we support.

12

Responsible Consumption and Production

Make more of every resource.

The plan connects efficient water use, better produce handling, and the conversion of organic by-products into biogas and fertiliser.

UN target 12.5Waste prevention and reuse↗
How progress could be measured

Organic material reused, fertiliser recovered, and changes in produce losses as facilities become operational.
