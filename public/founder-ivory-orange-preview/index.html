The person behind the purpose

A commitment that starts with people.

Iman Tadros founded Risemeup with a personal commitment to communities whose needs are too often overlooked.

Her work began with responding to need: supporting a food bank and homeless shelter in London during the pandemic, and coordinating food assistance for families in Sudan.

As a member of Sudan’s Coptic Orthodox community, she brings a personal understanding of the loss caused by war. Risemeup carries that commitment forward through support for both the Coptic Orthodox and Nuba Mountains communities.

Virgin Mary Farm was destroyed during the war. The land remains, and so does the ambition to rebuild a place of productive farming, learning and opportunity.
