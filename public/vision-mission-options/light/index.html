Vision & Mission.

Two Communities. One Vision. A Future of Hope.

I · THE FUTURE WE ENVISION

Our Vision

A future in which communities affected by war can live with dignity, access nutritious food and build secure livelihoods.

We envision productive land, shared opportunity and a place for people to learn, work and rebuild their lives.

From fragile land to thriving communities.

II · THE WORK THAT GUIDES US

Our Mission

To stand alongside Sudan’s Coptic Orthodox and Nuba Mountains communities through practical food assistance and long-term recovery.

Working with partners, we aim to rebuild Virgin Mary Farm through sustainable farming, clean energy, education and practical training.

Think globally, act locally.
